# SBC-7B2 R4 User-Facing Wording Impact Register

Date: 21 September 2026

R4 does not add a new user data activity, recipient, permission or storage location. Therefore no public privacy/help wording change is required solely because of the R4 reconciliation.

The eventual pre-release privacy/help material must still accurately explain the already-existing FT3/FT4 behaviours: local encrypted books, explicit statement imports, user-controlled document storage, local OCR/manual fallback, deterministic finite command text, and absence of Open Banking/remote accounting AI at this stage.

Do not publish claims such as “never leaves your device” globally until the final product-wide privacy audit has reconciled every later optional cloud/backup/diagnostic feature.
